# Progress Log

A one-page, phone-first log for daily progress across categories and subcategories.

- Score each entry from +3 to −3 (0 = neutral). Entries start at +1 unless a subcategory sets its own default.
- Categories with subcategories — Vitality (Sleep, Fitness, Weight, Grooming, Other), Stewardship (Money, Home, Family), Leadership, Work — all editable in Setup.
- Quick entries fill in text and points in one tap (e.g. Payday update +1 on the 5th and 20th, Missed payment −2, Clean/Dishes +1, Trash +1 on Wednesdays, Arrange babysitter +2).
- History with filters and search; tap ✎ on any entry to change its points, category, text or date, or delete it.
- Progress shows net points, a 12-week chart, and per-category/subcategory totals.

## Use it

Turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root), open the Pages URL on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen.

## Your data

Everything is stored in your browser on that device (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Setup → Download backup** regularly, and **Restore** to move your log to another phone or browser. **Download CSV** exports for spreadsheets.
